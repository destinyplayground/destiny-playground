Northern Europe · 9 Zones

“This is where it all started. Some of the oldest Fallen fortifications on Earth are deep here. They were here before we woke you up.”
- Ghost, on approach to the eastern EDZ

Overview

The eastern reaches of the European Dead Zone cover the former industrial heartland of northern Europe - forest, ruin, and contested ground stretching back from the coast. The Fallen arrived in the decades after the Traveler went silent and made it their own, fortifying the old townships and rail lines. More recently, Hive incursions have been reported pushing up through old mineshafts and tunnel networks toward the surface - a development the Vanguard is watching closely.

▲
Enemy Factions
The Fallen
The House of Kings have a presence across the eastern EDZ. Expect infantry, captains, and heavier support units deeper into their territory.
The Hive
A recent and unwelcome development - Hive have been sighted surfacing through old mine tunnels near Salt Mines and Hangman's Pass. Presence is currently limited, but growing.

Overview

The western EDZ is heavier, harder ground - the Cabal of the Red Legion have established firebase operations across the reaches, representing a significantly tougher threat than the Fallen presence further east. Fallen scavengers still work the margins, particularly around the waterline near Sunken Isles, but this territory belongs to the Cabal in force.

▲
Enemy Factions
The Cabal
Red Legion firebase operations dominate the western EDZ. Heavy armour, aerial support, and fortified positions. Higher threat than the Fallen presence.
The Fallen
Scavenger bands work the fringes of Cabal territory, picking over the wreckage the Legion leaves behind. Rarely engage the Cabal directly - too costly.

Jupiter's frozen moon conceals an ocean beneath ancient ice - and beneath that, things the Bray Corporation studied and never fully explained. The Fallen have established a significant presence near the ruins of the Bray Exoscience facility. The Vex have constructed gate networks across the surface. Overview

The Moon belongs to the Hive. Centuries before the Collapse, Hive broods tunnelled into the lunar crust, carving out their domain in a network of tunnels that extends for hundreds of kilometres. The surface is contested. Below the Hellmouth's lip, things are considerably worse. House of Exile Fallen have also established footholds on the surface - they avoid the Hive tunnels entirely.

▲

The Hive
Ancient and deeply entrenched. The surface offers their lighter forces; the deeper you go, the more powerful the enemies become.
The Fallen
House of Exile survivors occupy parts of the surface. They stay well away from Hive territory and have claimed a few Golden Age structures as their own.

Overview

Mars was the most active Golden Age colony before the Collapse. The Cabal arrived shortly after and methodically established control across the Meridian Bay. Two Cabal legions have a significant presence here. The Vex operate in parallel - their gate network runs beneath Cabal territory, and in The Buried City the two factions are in active conflict.

The Vex gate network runs beneath Cabal territory. Conflict between the two factions is active in several zones. Neither is your ally.

Overview

The Last City is humanity's final standing urban settlement, built in the shadow of the Traveler. The Guardian District and Tower serve as the social hub for Guardians - a central point for operations, vendors, and community. More details will be shared at launch.

■
Areas
Guardian District Courtyard
Social Area

The central gathering point of the Guardian District, nestled among the City's rooftops. The first stop for most Guardians passing through - open, busy, and always within sight of the Traveler.

Tower Courtyard
Social Area

The heart of the Tower - vendors, Vanguard leadership, and the constant hum of Guardians coming and going. If there's one place that defines what the Tower is, this is it.

Tower North
Social Area

The quieter northern reach of the Tower, overlooking the City below. Less foot traffic than the Courtyard, favoured by Guardians who want a moment away from the crowd.

Tower Hangar
