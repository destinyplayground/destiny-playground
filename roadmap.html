Early Access
Founder Access Period
TBA - 2026

Before the full launch, Founders get first access to the server. This is a feedback-focused phase - limited in scope by design, with no campaign, a minimal weapon pool, and a small selection of destinations and activities. Your input here shapes what ships at launch.

  • Exclusive to Founders
  • Core systems available for testing
  • Limited destinations and activities
  • Direct feedback pipeline to the team

Season 1: The Beginning

The full launch. The server opens to everyone with the complete main campaign, all core systems live, and the first full season of content.

  • Main campaign - all 6 Acts
  • All 4 launch destinations

  • Season 1 pass and rewards track
  • Triumphs, Titles and Fishing at launch
