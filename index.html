On June 9th, 2026, Destiny 2 receives its final update. After twelve years, the lights go down on one of the most extraordinary universes ever built in gaming. The worlds, the lore, the feeling of running a Raid with your Fireteam at midnight - none of that disappears. It lives on in everyone who was ever a Guardian.

Destiny Playground exists because of what Bungie built. This is our way of carrying it forward.
