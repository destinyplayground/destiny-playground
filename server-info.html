Expected Conduct
Server Rules

These rules apply to all players at all times, both in-game and in the Discord. They exist to keep Destiny Playground a good place to play. Breaking them results in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent removal depending on severity.

You can view the rules in-game at any time. Ignorance of the rules is not an excuse.

01
Respect Everyone
Treat all players, staff, and community members with basic respect. Harassment, hate speech, targeted abuse, and discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated. Disagreements happen — handle them like an adult.
Zero Tolerance
02
No Griefing or Theft
Do not destroy, modify, or steal from other players' builds or belongings without explicit permission. This includes anything in the world that doesn't belong to you. The server has logging. We will know.
Bannable
03
No Cheating or Exploiting
Hacked clients, cheat software, and deliberate exploitation of bugs or glitches to gain an unfair advantage are not allowed. If you find a bug, report it in #bug-reports — don't use it.
Bannable
04
No Story Spoilers
Destiny Playground has an original story. Do not spoil story events, mission outcomes, lore reveals, or character fates for players who haven't experienced them yet. Use spoiler tags in Discord when in doubt.
Warning / Mute
05
Keep Chat Clean
Excessive profanity, spamming, flooding chat, or using the in-game chat for advertisements is not welcome. Keep it readable and on-topic. This is a community space.
Warning / Mute
06
No Impersonation
Do not impersonate staff members, other players, or the Destiny Playground team in any context. This includes usernames, skins, or behaviour designed to mislead others.
Bannable
07
Respect the Experience
Don't intentionally ruin the experience for others — this includes blocking paths, trapping players, repeatedly killing new players without cause, or otherwise being a nuisance in ways not covered above.
Warning / Kick
08
Follow Staff Instructions
Staff decisions are final in the moment. If you disagree with a decision, raise it calmly through the proper channels — not by arguing in chat, ignoring instructions, or escalating. Staff are volunteers doing their best.
Required
ⓘ

These rules are subject to change. Significant updates will be announced in the Discord. Staff reserve the right to take action against behaviour that violates the spirit of these rules even if not explicitly listed.

Useful Info
Good to Know
■
This is a Fan Project
Destiny Playground is an independent fan-made server. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Bungie, Inc. in any way. Destiny and all related names are the property of Bungie.
◆
Progress is Saved Per Player
Your character, inventory, quest progress, and lore unlocks are saved server-side and tied to your Minecraft account. Switching accounts means starting over.
△
Discord is the Source of Truth
For the most up-to-date announcements, patch notes, downtime warnings, and community news, the Discord is always first. The website follows but the Discord is faster.
▶
Staff are Volunteers
The team behind Destiny Playground are unpaid volunteers building this in their spare time. Be patient, be kind, and consider supporting the project on Ko-fi if you enjoy it.
□
Seasons & Updates
Content is released in seasons, each bringing new story missions, destinations, and features. Season schedules are announced in the Discord and tracked on the Roadmap.
◆
Mods are Optional
The server runs entirely on plugins — you can play vanilla with zero mods installed. Official modpacks are available for performance and visual improvements. See the Getting Started page.
