Bug Reports

Found something broken? Fill out the form below. It builds a ready-to-send report for our Discord — just copy and paste it into #bug-reports. The more detail you can give, the faster we can fix it.

▲

Bug abuse is bannable. If you find a bug that gives an unfair advantage, report it immediately and do not use it. Deliberate exploitation — no matter how small — will result in a ban with no exceptions. See the Server Rules for full details.

ⓘ

Reporting through Discord's #bug-reports channel is the only official way to report bugs. Reports sent elsewhere (DMs, other channels, social media) may not be seen, tracked, or actioned. This form makes that process as quick as possible.
