About the role

We're looking for an experienced pixel artist to create 2D armour textures for both players and enemies, faithful to Destiny's visual language and adapted for Minecraft's scale and aesthetic. This is a skilled, detail-oriented role. You should be confident working within an established style and producing consistent, high-quality results independently.

This form should take approximately 20–40 minutes. There is no time limit, so please take your time. Applications cannot be edited after submission.

Requirements — all must be met to progress
  • Strong portfolio of previous pixel art work (required)
  • Experience creating 2D textures or sprite work — armour, clothing, or character skins preferred
  • Access to Destiny 1 and/or Destiny 2 for visual reference
  • Familiarity with Destiny's armour design and aesthetic
  • Age 16+
Please note

Destiny-inspired past work will significantly increase your chances of acceptance. If you don't have an existing portfolio, you may still apply — if you meet all other requirements, we may invite you to complete a short test piece (such as texturing a piece of Destiny-inspired armour or an enemy surface) before a final decision is made.

Responses generated using AI will not be considered.

Applications are reviewed within 1–3 weeks. We'll reach out via Discord DM. Please ping @CNT Darthgaming on Discord after submitting so we know your application is in.
