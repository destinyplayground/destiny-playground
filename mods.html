Setup and Installation
How to Install
What launcher do I need?
These packs are built for Fabric. Use a launcher with Fabric support - Modrinth App, ATLauncher, or Prism Launcher are all good options. The Modrinth App handles installs in one click.
Will mods affect my vanilla server?
No. All mods in these packs are strictly client-side. They don't change any server behaviour or affect other players in any way.
What if something breaks?
Head to #mod-support in the Discord and describe the issue. Include your pack version, Minecraft version, and what happened.
Community
Questions and Suggestions
Can I suggest a mod to include?
Yes - post in #suggestions on Discord. The packs are updated periodically and community suggestions do get reviewed.
Are mods allowed that aren't in these packs?
Client-side performance or visual mods are generally fine. Anything that provides a gameplay advantage, automates actions, or modifies server interactions is not permitted. When in doubt, ask in Discord before using it.
How often are packs updated?
Packs aren't updated on a fixed schedule - updates happen when Minecraft versions or included mods require it. Check the download pages for the latest versions.
