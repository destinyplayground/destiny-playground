Community Research

Help Shape Destiny Playground

We run periodic community surveys to guide development, gathering opinions on gameplay systems, content, and long-term direction straight from players. Every response feeds directly into what gets built. Below are our current and past surveys.

How Our Surveys Work

  • Open to everyone, including non-Destiny players unless stated otherwise
  • Every question is optional - answer as much or as little as you like
  • Fully anonymous unless you choose to share contact info
  • Used solely for internal design & development research
  • Data is never shared outside of the staff team
  • You can complete a survey any time it's marked as open

Data & Privacy

All responses are anonymous unless you voluntarily provide contact information. Your answers are used solely for design and development research purposes, and data is never shared outside of the staff team.

If you'd like to retract your details after completing a survey, email us at thedestinyplayground@gmail.com and we'll delete your user details.

Want the survey link the moment a new one drops? Join our Discord server - new surveys are always announced and pinned there first.
