Good To Know Before You Apply

This is a volunteer project and the environment is relaxed - but communication is what keeps it running. Let us know when you're unable to work; extended or repeated inactivity may result in removal from the team at any point.

All roles begin with roughly a one-month probationary period, during which we're assessing skills and fit and may remove someone if it's not working out. After that, you're in.

Responses generated with AI assistance will not be considered for any role, at any stage of the application.

Open Roles

What The Role Involves

You'll be building out the technical side of the server - coding weapons, armour, items, enemies, missions and the systems that hold everything together. This is a coding role rather than a design one, so clear, regular communication with the lead is essential to avoid work clashing or duplicating.

You'll also be expected to diagnose and fix bugs as they come up, and this role continues on past launch - there's ongoing work in maintaining and expanding what's already built.

What The Role Involves

Creating 2D armour textures for players and enemies within Minecraft, consistent with Destiny's visual direction. This means working faithfully from Destiny reference material - translating real armour sets, materials, and colour schemes into Minecraft's texture format without losing what makes them recognisable.

You'll be producing a steady stream of these across both player and enemy models, so textures need to hold up consistently alongside everyone else's work, not just as one-off standout pieces.
