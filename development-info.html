Destiny Playground is still in active development and isn't playable yet - there's no IP to join, no client to download, nothing to install. This page exists so you know exactly where things actually stand, and how to follow along until that changes.

01
Where Things Stand
Current Status

Destiny Playground is being built by a small team, largely in the open, one system at a time. Right now that means things like abilities, weapons, the campaign, and destinations are all in various stages of progress - some further along than others - but nothing is complete, and there's no playable build available.

◆

There's no release date being promised here. Development pace varies, and we'd rather be honest about that than give a date we can't guarantee.

The most accurate picture of what's actually done, in progress, or upcoming is the Roadmap - it's kept up to date and is a better source of truth than any single page of text could be.

Stay In The Loop
Follow Along

Progress gets shared regularly through a few channels, depending on how much detail you want:

◆

◇
Roadmap - the clearest at-a-glance view of what's done, what's next, and roughly when. Check the Roadmap page directly.
□
Discord - the most active place for day-to-day updates, previews, and direct conversation with the team.
▶
YouTube and social channels - trailers, dev insight videos, and clips get posted here as they release.
03
Want To Help?
Get Involved

The team is small and always open to the right people - builders, modellers, artists, coders, composers, and more. If you've got relevant skills and want to contribute, applications and details are handled through the Discord.

If you're not sure whether what you can offer fits, ask anyway. We'd rather hear from you and find out than not at all.

04
Before You Ask
Questions

A lot of common questions - about the project, the team, timelines, and how things work - are already answered on the FAQ page, and it's kept up to date alongside dev updates. Worth checking there first.

▲

A note on scams: Destiny Playground is not open to join. If anyone claims otherwise, offers you early access, or asks for payment or personal information to "get in," it is not us. Report it in the Discord.
