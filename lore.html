Mysteries of the Age

Mystery — Priority One
What is the Darkness, and why did it return?
The Darkness came once before, and the Traveler sacrificed itself to stop it — or so the story goes. But the Traveler's silence since that sacrifice raises a different question: did it sacrifice itself, or was it wounded? The Darkness is returning in fragments now. Not a fleet. Not an army. Something quieter. Something that seems to be watching, testing, waiting for the right moment to reveal what it actually wants.
Status: Unknown

What Did Rasputin Do at the Collapse?
The Warmind Rasputin was the most powerful AI ever built — a global defense network that went silent during the Collapse. The records that survive suggest it chose not to fight. Or perhaps chose to fight something else entirely. Its bunkers are still sealed. Its guns still track things across the sky. It is not dead. It is simply not talking.

Ghost: Origin & Purpose
Ref. GH-001 — Level 3 Clearance
Archive
001

The Traveler created the Ghosts in the last moments before it went silent. What it knew then — what compelled it to spend what remained of its Light on billions of tiny floating intelligences — no one has answered. The Ghosts themselves do not know.

Each Ghost carries a directive: find the one they were meant to find. This is not a metaphor. The bond between a Guardian and their Ghost is not transferable, not replaceable, and not fully understood. A Ghost who has found their Guardian will protect them with everything they are. A Ghost who hasn't found theirs yet will search for centuries if necessary.

In Destiny Playground, your Ghost is your companion, your scanner, your resurrection anchor, and the closest thing you have to someone who is genuinely on your side in a solar system that mostly isn't. Take care of them.

Vanguard Archive — Cosmology
The Traveler
Ref. TV-001 — Level 4 Clearance
Archive
002

It arrived without warning. A white sphere the size of a small moon, hanging silently in the orbit of Mars. Within decades, the Martian permafrost had receded, revealing oceans. Within a century, Venus had breathable air. The Traveler transformed worlds. It gave humanity access to technology centuries beyond what they could have developed alone.

This period is called the Golden Age. Humanity spread across the solar system. Colonies flourished. Lifespans extended. And then the Darkness came, following the Traveler like a shadow that had finally caught up with the thing casting it.

The Traveler fought back. And in doing so, it was wounded so severely that it has been silent ever since — hovering above what remains of the Last City, its shell cracked, its voice lost. Whether it is healing, sleeping, or simply gone in some meaningful sense that doesn't register to those watching from below, no one can say for certain.

Vanguard Archive — History
The Collapse
Ref. CL-001 — Level 5 Clearance
Archive
003

Records from before the Collapse are fragmentary. What remains paints a picture of a civilization that had no warning and no recourse. The Darkness did not negotiate. It did not make demands. Colonies went dark. Ships went silent. The Warmind Rasputin fired everything it had and it wasn't enough.

In the final moments, the Traveler acted. A pulse of Light, enormous and final-seeming, pushed the Darkness back. It worked. Barely. Humanity survived, reduced to a single city on a single planet, huddled beneath a wounded god that could no longer speak to them.

The Darkness did not leave. It retreated. It watched. And over the centuries since, it has been sending scouts. Testing defenses. Testing Guardians. The Collapse was not the end of the war. It was the intermission.

Vanguard Archive — Paracausal Forces
The Light
Ref. LT-001 — Level 3 Clearance
Archive
004

The Light is not magic. It is not science, either, in any framework that survived the Collapse. It is something older than both — a paracausal force that exists outside the normal rules of cause and effect. It resurrects the dead. It allows Guardians to throw fire with their bare hands. It makes the impossible into a Tuesday afternoon.

The Traveler is the source, or at least the conduit. Ghosts channel it to their Guardians. Guardians wield it through their subclass — Solar, Arc, or Void, each a different expression of the same underlying force, shaped by the Guardian's will and purpose.

What the Light cannot do is answer questions. It does not explain itself. Warlocks have spent lifetimes studying it and arrived at frameworks that are useful without being complete. The Light gives Guardians their power. The rest — what to do with it, whether it deserves to be trusted — that part is on you.
