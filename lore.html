Known Destinations

Planets & Worlds

Four destinations confirmed across the solar system, each occupied or contested by forces older than human memory. Two social spaces serve as the backbone of Guardian civilization — what little of it remains.

Earth
European Dead Zone · The Northreach
Earth is fractured but not finished. The forests of the European Dead Zone have reclaimed the ruins with something that feels almost intentional — as if the wilderness is holding its breath. Further north, the Northreach stretches into frozen wastes where pre-Collapse military infrastructure rusts beneath the ice. Earth is where the story begins. It is not where it ends.

Social Space — Guardian Hub
The Farm
Hidden in a dense stretch of the European Dead Zone, the Farm is what's left of Guardian civilization when no one is performing for anyone. It is not grand. It is a collection of repurposed structures around a working water wheel — a place where survivors chose to keep building instead of waiting to die. Come here to resupply, collect bounties, connect with Vanguard contacts, and remember what you're actually fighting for.

The Vanguard
“The three who chose to stay”
When the City fell and the Speaker went silent, the Vanguard did not retreat. Three Guardians — one Hunter, one Warlock, one Titan — took the chairs in the Tower and held the line not for glory but because someone had to. They coordinate strikes, issue bounties, manage fragile alliances, and make decisions that no one fully agrees with. They are not perfect. They are present. In a solar system that has lost almost everything, that counts for more than it should.

The Survivors
“The ones without Light who kept going anyway”
Not everyone who survived was a Guardian. Most were farmers, engineers, medics, children. They found the Farm in the European Dead Zone and built something out of nothing. They fix things. They grow things. They argue about resource allocation and quietly about whether Guardians actually understand what they're protecting. They are the reason “protecting humanity” is more than an abstraction. Some of them would very much like Guardians to occasionally remember that.

Enemy — Eliksni Houses
The Fallen
“Once-chosen, now desperate”
The Eliksni were once blessed by the Traveler. Their legends call it the Great Machine — a golden age taken without warning or explanation. When the Traveler left them, their civilization fractured into warring Houses: Kings, Devils, Wolves, Salvation. Each fighting for ether, for dominance, for whatever edge the scraps of human technology might provide. There is tragedy underneath the aggression. The Fallen lost what humanity is still desperately trying to protect. That doesn't make them safe. It makes them comprehensible. Active on Earth and Europa.

Enemy — Sword Logic Cult
The Hive
“The worm-god's tithe”
The Hive do not simply want to kill you. They want to prove something: that existence is owed only to those strong enough to take and hold it. This is the Sword Logic — an ancient philosophy delivered by worm gods, practiced across millions of years. Every Guardian slain becomes a sacrament. Every ritual, a deeper communion with the Darkness. The Hellmouth on the Moon is not a base. It is a wound cut by faith, and something at the bottom of it has been receiving offerings for three hundred years. Active on the Moon.

Enemy — Synthetic Collective
The Vex
“The question with no answer”
The Vex are not a civilization. They are a process — a self-replicating, time-manipulating computational system with a singular objective that no one has decoded. They construct gate-networks across planetary surfaces. They edit their own history. They have simulated entire civilizations and deleted them when the results were unsatisfactory. They do not hate Guardians. They have simply identified Guardians as a variable that disrupts their calculations, and they are working on the solution. They are very patient. They have a lot of time. Active on Europa.

The Cabal
“The empire that never stops expanding”
The Cabal are a military civilization so large that the legions on Mars haven't received a homeworld transmission in decades and have continued operating anyway on standing orders. They came for the Traveler. They've been methodically dismantling solar system infrastructure ever since — not out of malice but out of protocol. This is what the Cabal do. They arrive, they assess, they conquer. Legionaries hold lines. Psions bend probability. Colossi walk like siege engines. They are not monsters. They are soldiers. That is almost worse. Active on Mars.

Guardian Classes

The Traveler chose carefully. Or perhaps it chose everyone and these are the ones the Light decided to keep. Three classes. Three philosophies. One shared purpose — or at least, that's the working theory.

Hunter
Image Slot
Hunter
Pathfinder · Survivor · Lone Wolf
Hunters were wanderers before they were Guardians. They mapped the wilds, scavenged in places no one else went, and survived on instinct alone. That hasn't changed. They move fast, commit late, and trust their own read of a situation over anyone else's briefing. In Destiny Playground, Hunters excel at mobility, precision damage, and solo engagements. They don't always play well with others. That's a known issue.

Unanswered Questions

Mysteries of the Age

Some things in this universe do not have explanations yet. Some may never. These are the open questions — the threads that Destiny Playground's lore is built around pulling on.

Mystery — Priority One
What is the Darkness, and why is it returning?
The Darkness came once before and the Traveler stopped it — or wounded itself stopping it, which is not the same thing. The Darkness retreated. It did not leave. It has been patient ever since, watching, probing, sending things ahead of itself to assess what resistance remains. Whether it is preparing a second attempt or simply waiting for the Light to exhaust itself is not yet known.
Status: Active Investigation — Priority One
I
Mystery — Warmind
What did the Warmind decide during the Collapse?
Earth's defense network was the last line before the Traveler itself. During the Collapse, the Warmind went silent. The records suggest it made a choice in those final moments — not to fight the Darkness directly, but to do something else entirely. Its bunkers remain sealed. Its guns still track objects in the sky. Whatever it decided, it has not explained itself to anyone who has asked.
Status: Sealed — Authorization Required
II
Mystery — Clovis Bray
What did Bray build beneath Europa?
The Bray Exoscience facility ran experiments classified above Vanguard clearance levels during the Golden Age. The Deep Stone Crypt is somewhere beneath it. Exo Guardians report recurring identical dreams: a black orb, a long descent, something that taught them to forget. House of Salvation has been inside for months. Something in sub-level seven has convinced them not to go lower. Nobody knows what that something is.
Status: Active Site — Unknown Threat
III
Mystery — The Nine
Who are the Nine, and what do they want?
They send Emissaries. They observe the Trials. They offer rewards without conditions. The Nine are something — ancient intelligences, dark matter given consciousness, echoes of the first humans to reach the outer solar system. Nobody agrees on what. What is agreed: they know things they shouldn't, and when they speak, it is worth listening even when you cannot determine whether they're on your side.
Status: Deeply Unresolved
IV
Mystery — The Traveler
Why did the Traveler choose humanity?
It came to Mars. It terraformed worlds. It said nothing. Of all the species in the solar system it could have uplifted — and there were others, before the Collapse — it chose humanity. The Eliksni believe it chose them first, and then abandoned them. That belief has fueled centuries of war. What the Traveler actually intended has never been explained. Whether it is capable of explanation is its own open question.
Status: Unanswered — Origin Unknown
V
Mystery — The Ghosts
How did the Traveler know who to make Ghosts for?
Each Ghost was created for a specific Guardian. This is not metaphor — Ghosts search for centuries, cross continents, sift through ruins, and know when they've found the right person. How the Traveler encoded that knowledge before going silent, and what it saw in each Guardian worth preserving, has no current explanation. The Ghosts themselves report they simply know. That answer satisfies no one, including the Ghosts.
Status: No Framework Available
