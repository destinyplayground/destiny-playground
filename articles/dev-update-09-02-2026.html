First Destination Reveal
Earth

Today, we're revealing the first destination on the Director: Earth. Earth is a multi-location destination, meaning it contains more than one distinct playable area, including:

  • The EDZ (European Dead Zone)
  • A separate location where the tutorial takes place - Northreach

Rather than placing both of these directly on the main Director, Earth acts as a container destination. Selecting Earth opens a sub-selection screen, allowing players to choose where on the planet they want to deploy.

Earth sub-selection screen on the Director

You may also notice activity nodes already present on the Director, such as Vanguard Operations and Crucible. These are not final implementations, and more information on these systems will come in future dev updates once their designs are further along.

The Director

If you're familiar with Destiny or Destiny 2, the concept of the Director should feel immediately recognisable. In The Destiny Playground, the Director serves as the primary way to access destinations and activities, acting as the connection between all major gameplay systems.

The Director allows players to:

  • View all available destinations
  • Access activity playlists (such as Vanguard Operations or the Crucible)
  • Understand progression requirements
  • Navigate the game world

World View

Selecting a planet in the Director does not immediately teleport you. Instead, it opens a world-view screen for that destination. From there, players choose a specific landing zone. This allows us to avoid cluttering the main Director with too many nodes, support destinations with multiple playable locations, and expand destinations over time without redesigning the UI.

Each landing zone displays whether it is a Patrol Zone or Social Space, and its level requirement.
