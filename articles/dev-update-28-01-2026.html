What Are Titles?

Titles are mastery identifiers that players can earn and equip once they've completed a specific set of requirements.

Once earned, a Title:

  • Is permanently unlocked on your account
  • Can be equipped and displayed at any time
  • Acts as proof of mastery in a particular area of the game

Unlike Triumphs, Titles are not about total completion - they're about focus and commitment to an area.

Titles vs Triumphs

Although related, Titles and Triumphs are separate systems.

  • Triumphs track everything you do and reward steady progress
  • Titles represent mastery in a specific area

A Title might draw from multiple systems:

  • Combat challenges
  • Activity completions
  • Long-term objectives
  • Difficult or skill-based feats

This separation lets Triumphs remain broad and accessible, while Titles stay aspirational and prestigious.

How Titles Are Earned

Each Title has a unique set of objectives tied to it.

These objectives can include:

  • Completing specific activities under certain conditions
  • Finishing difficult encounters solo or flawless
  • Repeatedly engaging with a particular activity type
  • Achieving milestones tied to skill or knowledge

Not all objectives are visible from the start. Some will only reveal themselves as you progress, rewarding exploration and discovery.

No One-Size-Fits-All

Titles are intentionally varied in difficulty and focus.

Some Titles may be:

  • Combat-heavy
  • Exploration-focused
  • Activity-specific
  • Long-term commitment based

This means that you are not expected to chase every Title - different playstyles are equally valid paths to mastery.

Permanent, Earned, and Meaningful

Once you earn a Title, it's yours permanently. Titles:

  • Do not expire
  • Are not seasonal resets
  • Do not require re-earning

If you see a Title from an earlier era of the server, it means that player was there and completed the requirements when they were available.

The Future of the System

The Title system is built to scale with the server. This allows us to:

  • Add new Titles over time
  • Introduce Titles tied to new systems or activities
  • Create variants of Titles

Titles at Launch

At launch, Titles will be:

  • Limited in number
  • Focused on core gameplay
  • Designed to introduce players to the system naturally

As the server grows, so will the variety and ambition of Titles available.
