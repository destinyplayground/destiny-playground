The Lost D2MC Story: Season of Dissonance

11 Jan, 2026
CNT Darthgaming Behind the Scenes

Hello everyone! A little surprise drop right here.

For those of you that are fans of D2MC, you may know that I was running my own storyline over there, and many of you were fans of it. As I'm sure - just like me - you were disappointed to learn that the story would be stopping, and that I would not be continuing it here in favour of starting a fresh new and improved story. Well, today is your lucky day.

The following article is taken from the 11-page document that was made (by that point) to prepare to create the next D2MC story: Season of the Dissonance.

A Few Notes

At the end of this article there is also a video released at the very start of the season - a showcase of the UI menus made for it (redacted at the time, as I didn't know about the story then).

Those of you in the D2MC community may recognise the name Dissonance. Many easter eggs were already in place - the only one I recall is them starting to spell it out at the end of Vex Network guides.

Not mentioned in this, there were plans for old D2MC events to be remade and come back here (I was 2/3 of the way done with a complete overhaul of the Convergence event). Maybe if the hype is there post-launch, we'll re-remaster it for DP?

Additionally, the space for most of the entire first mission had already been made, including changing the entire Firebase Hades for this mission, with plans to further revamp it following the season. Let me know if you want screenshots and I will find them!

Below is the document, copied 1-to-1, just reformatted to fit in this article.

Main Plot

A year has passed since Ehkazier, Disciple of the Witness, fell. But his influence lingers. House Lost, once scattered and leaderless, now rallies under a hidden Kell - one whose name is spoken only in code. With Demiks at their side, they have recovered the Stabilization Reports: Ehkazier's forbidden blueprints for a timeline where the Witness triumphed, the Traveler is gone, and the Eliksni thrive in eternal darkness.

You return to a fractured system. Mithrax, along with unexpected allies, races to stop House Lost before the Reports can be activated. The risk? A rewritten reality echoing the horrors Ehkazier once vowed to bring. Time is splintering. Will you restore the path of light - or be dragged into a future that was never meant to be?

Missions

Mission I
Distortion

An Eliksni warband from House Lost has launched a full-scale assault on Firebase Hades. The Cabal are scrambling, reports of temporal anomalies are spreading, and something deep beneath the EDZ is beginning to stir. Join Mithrax in investigating the conflict before it escalates any further.

  • Intro - "Investigate Firebase Hades" Player loads into a heavily reworked Firebase Hades. Fires rage, Cabal vs. Eliksni warfare all around. Dialogue plays (Ghost + Mithrax). Players fight through Cabal chaos to get past the new wall.
  • "Clear the Warzone" Encounter both Cabal and House Lost Eliksni. First encounter with House Lost Baron - fight 1/3 of his health before retreat. Regular way into Firebase Hades has collapsed; must go through the overhauled "Overtaken Enclave" Lost Sector toward The Winding Cove.
  • "Push Toward the Collapse Site" Scenery shifts drastically - chunks are glitched into a temporal state. Anomaly effects kick in: slowed movement, Ghost's voice distorts briefly.
  • "Scan the Temporal Distortion" Ghost scans a glowing tear near the rubble. Brief flashback: ghostly silhouettes of Cabal and Eliksni fighting in reverse. Players fight through more enemies on the path to the Winding Cove.
  • "Pursue the Source into Winding Cove" Players arrive in the Winding Cove - Eliksni skiffs drop in more enemies. Midway through, Ghost detects life signatures inside a sealed cave.
  • "Secure the Den" - Boss Encounter Reach Axtrazia's Den (newly accessible area from Winding Cove). Several waves of House Lost Eliksni, ending with the final fight with the House Lost Baron. The door blasts open - reveal: Axtrazia, alive, cloaked in temporal distortion.
Rewards: Unlocks Chronopass Pages 1-6 • Drops Personal Quest I
Mission II
Cold Reclaim

After a lead from a defector points to a missing report on Europa, Guardians travel to the icy moon to find it. But Salvation's forces - now under the command of a ruthless Archon - are one step ahead.

  • Intro - "Meet with The Defector" Player meets with The Defector. Mid-conversation, they are ambushed by the Archon Priest of House Salvation, who confirms the report's existence before fleeing. Hints that Salvation is exploiting House Lost's chaos for their own gain.
  • "Pursue House Salvation" Skirmishes across Europa featuring House Salvation, House Dusk, and House Lost patrols along the way.
  • "Pursue The Archon Priest through the structure" Player fights through the structure until arriving at the Archon Priest's location.
  • "Defeat the lieutenants of Houses Dusk and Lost" The Archon vanishes - player fights a House Dusk and House Lost lieutenant, simultaneously or one-by-one.
  • "Defeat Aksor, Archon Priest of House Salvation" Player battles the Archon Priest at the site where Eramis was defeated in D2, before the Archon flees to their Ketch with the report.
  • "Sabotage The Ketch's Engines" Player follows onto the Ketch, fighting through 4 rooms to disable 3 engines via terminals.
  • "Defeat Aksor - Final Encounter" Final fight with enhanced attacks. Lights flickering. Report recovered.
  • "Retrieve The Report" Dialogue reveals the Archon had partial insight into Ehkazier's timeline manipulation, thinking it could benefit House Salvation. Ikora and/or Mithrax remarks on how close the enemy is to weaponising the missing reports.
Rewards: Unlocks Chronopass Pages 19-24 • Drops Personal Quest II

As mentioned at the start, this story will sadly never come to fruition - but that is not to say that elements of it, and what it stood for in terms of goals and ideas for the player experience, won't make their way into TDP. I really look forward to putting together a well-thought-out experience that you can tell has truly been cared for.
