Developer Update Roadmap

Seasons

A Season in The Destiny Playground represents a defined period of time where:

  • Progression is refreshed
  • New rewards become available
  • The server has a clear thematic and gameplay focus
  • Updates and content releases

Seasons aren't just cosmetic refreshes - they act to give the server added progression, with moments where new systems, activities, or changes can be added, while still allowing players to engage at their own pace. Seasons also help define eras of the server. When you look back, each season may feel distinct, both mechanically and aesthetically.

The Season Pass - Core Vision

The Season Pass exists for three main reasons:

  • Long-Term Progression. No matter how you play - combat, exploration, activities, fishing, quests - you should always feel like your time is contributing toward something meaningful.
  • Sustainable Development. The server is free to access, but it is not free to run. A premium season pass provides a sustainable and meaningful way to support server costs and continued development without locking core gameplay behind paywalls.
  • Structured Updates. Seasons give us clear windows to release updates, test new ideas, and evolve the server in a controlled way.

Free Track vs Premium Track

Each season features a 100-tier Season Pass, split into two tracks:

Available to All
Free Track
  • In-game currency
  • Upgrade or utility materials
  • Select cosmetics
  • Meaningful rewards throughout the pass
Paid Upgrade
Premium Track
  • Exclusive cosmetics
  • Visual effects
  • Emotes or animations
  • Gear variants or themed items
  • Seasonal flair and progression showcase

The free track is not filler. Progressing through a season should feel worthwhile even if you never purchase the premium track.

"We want to be upfront about this early: Yes, the premium track will be paid. The server itself will remain completely free to access, and no gameplay power will be locked behind the premium pass, other than easier access to seasonal weapons and exotics."

The price of the premium pass may vary depending on the length of a season, ensuring that no season feels less valuable than another.

Seasonal Challenges

Seasonal Challenges act as larger, structured objectives that offer significant XP rewards. While these are still being designed, they will:

  • Cover a wide range of activities
  • Encourage interaction with new seasonal content
  • Highlight newly introduced systems or features

Challenges are optional, but designed to feel rewarding rather than mandatory.

Rewards

At this stage, the exact rewards on the pass are still being finalised. However, reward categories may include cosmetics, utility items, materials, visual effects, and seasonal-themed gear or items.

On exclusivity - not all rewards will follow the same rules, as some items may:

  • Remain exclusive to the season pass
  • Return later via the Season Archive
  • Reappear through other gameplay avenues in the future

There is no universal guarantee that an item will or won't return, and likewise, no guarantee when it might. The season pass is always the most direct way to earn seasonal rewards while they're active.

The Season Archive

A newly introduced Destiny 2 feature that you may recognise! When a season ends, its pass will no longer be active. However, key rewards from that season will be added to the Season Archive.

Through the Season Archive:

  • Players may purchase access to past season passes at a reduced cost and then purchase key items that have returned via the archive
  • Players who already owned the pass may purchase key items that have returned via the archive

This system exists to reduce long-term FOMO while still preserving the identity of each season.

Season Timing and Progression

At server launch, no season pass will be active. This is intentional - we want players to have breathing room to experience the main campaign, learn core systems, and settle into the world without additional progression pressure.

The first season pass is currently planned to launch a few weeks to a month after server release. Once a season pass is live, access to the Season Pass UI and its rewards will be locked behind completion of the main campaign. This ensures a consistent narrative and onboarding experience.

Importantly:

  • Season XP will not begin tracking until the season officially starts
  • Once the season is active but the campaign is not complete, season XP and quest completions will still be tracked - you just won't be able to access them yet
  • Once the season is active and the campaign is completed, full season progression becomes available

Season Pass - Featured QandA

We've put together a quick FAQ to act as a summary, as well as to cover any missed avenues. As always, contact us via the discord server if you have any additional queries.

Why introduce a paid season pass at all?
The Destiny Playground will be completely free to access, but running and maintaining a live server comes with ongoing costs. A paid premium season pass allows us to support server hosting, development tools, and continued updates without locking core gameplay behind paywalls. The premium pass is designed as a way to support the project, not a requirement to enjoy it.
Does the premium season pass give gameplay advantages?
No. Season pass rewards - free or premium - will not provide direct gameplay power that creates an unfair advantage. Progression systems, activities, and core gameplay remain accessible to all players.
Can I play the server without ever buying a season pass?
Yes. All major systems, activities, and content are accessible without purchasing a premium pass. The free track provides meaningful rewards each season, and the server is designed to remain enjoyable without premium participation. It just may mean that certain seasonal weapons are easier to obtain via the season pass, or seasonal exotics will be significantly harder to obtain without it.
What happens if I join late in a season?
You can still progress the season pass at your own pace while the season is active. Depending on timing, some rewards may require dedicated playtime to unlock, but the Season Archive exists to reduce long-term loss of access to key items.
What exactly is the Season Archive?
The Season Archive is a system where key rewards from past seasons may become available after a season ends. Through the archive:
  • Players may purchase access to past season passes at a reduced cost and then purchase key items that have returned via the archive
  • Players who already owned the pass may purchase key items that have returned via the archive
Not every reward is guaranteed to return, and there's no fixed schedule for when archived items become available.
Are season pass rewards exclusive forever?
There is no single rule that applies to all rewards. Some items may:
  • Remain exclusive to the season pass
  • Return later via the Season Archive
  • Reappear through other gameplay avenues in the future
The season pass is always the most direct and reliable way to earn seasonal rewards while they're active.
Why lock season pass access behind campaign completion?
The main campaign is designed to introduce core systems, world context, and player progression foundations. Locking season pass access behind campaign completion ensures a smoother onboarding experience, narrative consistency, and that seasons feel like a continuation of the journey, not a replacement for it.
Will every season include new story content?
Not necessarily. Narrative content takes time to design, implement, and test. While future campaigns and story content are planned post-release, not every season will feature a full narrative arc. Some seasons may focus more on gameplay systems, activities, balance updates, or experimental features - or just give you some new items to chase.
Will seasons always be the same length?
Season length is still being finalised, but the current plan is for seasons to be similar in length to Destiny 2's. Pricing for the premium pass may adjust based on season duration to ensure consistent value.
Can content release outside of season launches?
Yes. Seasons provide a strong structure for updates, but content is not locked to season start dates. Updates may release whenever ready.
What's the current development priority?
Right now, the top priorities are:
  • Core systems
  • The main campaign
  • Server foundation
Other systems like this are being developed alongside these, but they are not the primary focus until launch readiness is achieved.
