What Are Triumphs?

The Triumph system functions similarly to Destiny 2's Triumphs, taking inspiration from both Triumphs and Medals. At its core, this system exists to:

  • Track your progress across every avenue of the server
  • Reward players for engaging with different systems
  • Provide long-term goals beyond regular progression

Triumphs cover a wide range of categories, including Guardian, Gear, Combat, Activities, Exploration, and more.

Whether you're a completionist, grinder, or someone who just likes seeing numbers go up, Triumphs are designed to always give you something to work toward.

Types of Triumphs

There are two main types of Triumphs:

Challenge Triumphs

Challenge Triumphs are one-time completions. These focus on singular objectives, notable accomplishments, and difficult or unique feats. They tend to be more rewarding, as they often represent a specific achievement.

Tiered Triumphs

Tiered Triumphs are multi-stage objectives, with rewards granted at each tier. These are typically broader in scope, more grind-focused, and designed to reward long-term dedication. Tiered Triumphs can have any number of tiers, and the system allows for new tiers to be added in the future, meaning existing Triumphs can continue evolving and updating.

As a bonus, all Triumphs display the global completion percentage, so you'll always know how rare (or common) your accomplishments are.

Some Triumphs may also be hidden, meaning you won't know they exist until you unlock them. Whether you discover them naturally or through community knowledge... that's part of the fun.

Triumph Rewards and Points

Completing Triumphs rewards Triumph Points. These points contribute toward your Triumph Rewards Track, a long-term reward system that unlocks increasingly valuable rewards as you progress.

Key details:

  • Rewards unlock at set Triumph Point thresholds
  • Each reward tier is more valuable than the last
  • There are currently 10 pages of rewards, reaching up to Level 240
  • Both reward pages and total levels can be expanded in the future as new Triumphs and categories are added

Some Triumphs may also grant immediate bonus rewards, especially for particularly difficult or noteworthy accomplishments. You can also view your total Triumph completion count, allowing you to track just how much of the system you've conquered.

Titles and Seals

You might be wondering: what about Titles and Seals?

These will exist separately from Triumphs. Rather than being tied to completing a specific set of Triumphs, Titles will be earned through completing difficult activities, repeating certain challenges a set number of times, and accomplishing notable feats across the game. More details on Titles and how they're earned will be shared in a future dev update.

Long-Term Goals

The Triumph system is designed to ensure that players always have something to work toward, are rewarded for engaging with different parts of the server, and can show off their dedication and accomplishments.

As with all systems shown so far, this is still early in development. Values, rewards, UI, and Triumph definitions may change as development continues and systems evolve.

Also This Week - ADS Weapon System

A quick feature note: the ADS system works by simply left-clicking to zoom in. When zoomed, the following is applied based on the zoom stat: level of zoom, decreased movement speed. You may also just find it easier to be accurate when in ADS.

Additionally, entering ADS does reload the weapon, but triggers the ready speed cooldown when entering or exiting ADS mode.
