Vendors

Almost all vendors on the server will be located within its social spaces, acting largely how you'd expect a Destiny-style vendor to function. They serve as access points for:

  • Gear and items
  • Quests and progression
  • Server systems and features
  • Seasonal or activity-specific content

A lot of care has gone - and will continue to go - into where vendors are placed within social spaces. Important or frequently used vendors are prioritised with easier access, while more specialised vendors are positioned in ways that make sense thematically and spatially. The goal is for vendors to feel intuitive to find, easy to return to, and clearly tied to their role, without overwhelming social spaces or cluttering them unnecessarily.

The Guide Vendor

If there's one vendor every player should know, it's The Guide.

The Guide Vendor is your number one stop if you're ever lost or unsure what to do next. They act as a central hub, allowing players to:

  • View recent server updates
  • Access the Guidebook (more on that below)
  • Report bugs or players
  • Join the Discord
  • Support the server

The Guide's menu uses a new type of Minecraft menu system only recently discovered, which makes navigating it significantly smoother and faster than traditional menus.

Looking Ahead
The Guide - Future Expansion

Looking ahead, The Guide may also expand into a system that helps point players toward activities they haven't tried yet, quests appropriate for their progression, and systems worth engaging with at different stages. This isn't fully planned yet, but it's very much the direction this vendor is intended to grow toward.

The Guidebook

The Guidebook is the official source for how everything on the server works. This includes:

  • Features and mechanics
  • Activities and systems
  • Gear types and progression
  • Anything that might otherwise require asking staff or digging through messages

If you're ever confused about how something works, the Guidebook is where you're expected to go first. There are currently no plans to run an official public wiki for The Destiny Playground - however, if the community creates one, we're more than happy to provide official support, help keep information accurate, and direct players to it, provided it stays reasonably up-to-date.

The Guidebook exists to ensure players always have access to clear, reliable information without needing external tools.

The Guidebook will be accessible both from The Guide Vendor and from your Menu.
