We're Expanding the Team

For the past couple of months I've been working on a full overhaul of how we recruit and structure the volunteer team here. A lot of this project has been running through me - development, direction, community, all of it - largely because it's still early and most of the ideas are locked in my head. That's not sustainable, especially with my final year of university starting in just a few weeks, where I'll need to direct most of my time toward that and my final project.

So the goal is simple: spread the work out, reduce the bottleneck of everything running through one person, and build something that can keep moving even when I can't be glued to it constantly.

We've gone through every existing role listing and updated the requirements and expectations, and added several roles that didn't exist before. All of it now lives on a dedicated Roles page rather than being scattered across Discord messages.

Here's a proper look at what's actually open right now:

Server Developer - 1/2 positions

This is a new one, and honestly one of the most important additions. You'd be coding the technical side of the server - weapons, armour, enemies, missions, and the systems holding it all together. Mostly working within .yml files and configuring existing plugins, but if you've got experience building custom plugins yourself, that's a huge plus for your application. This role also continues well past launch - there's ongoing maintenance and expansion work baked into it permanently.

Builder - 4/4 positions

Recreating Destiny locations in Minecraft, built to accurate scale and faithful to the source material, detailed enough to actually hold up in a live server environment. Initial assignment options are The Last City, the EDZ, the Moon, or Mars - though you may be moved between locations later, nothing's fixed in stone. Builds leaning on import tools or Axiom alone won't be accepted; we want work that shows real hand-built skill.

Modeller - 1/1 position

Modelling and animating enemies in Blockbench, to accurate scale and consistent with everything else already built. If you can also model weapons, or you've got MythicMobs/Nexo knowledge to help implement your own enemies directly into the server, both are strong bonuses on top of the base role.

Pixel Artist - Armour and Textures - 1/1 position

Creating 2D armour textures for players and enemies, working faithfully from Destiny reference material - translating real armour sets, materials, and colour schemes into Minecraft's texture format without losing what makes them recognisable. This one's a steady stream of work rather than one-off pieces, so consistency matters as much as individual quality.

Pixel Artist - UI and Menus - 1/1 position

The other new pixel art role, focused entirely on interface work - menu backgrounds, icons, buttons, inventory and GUI elements - rather than character or enemy assets. Worth being upfront: there isn't a huge amount of work for this role right now, since everything's still changing rapidly, but that opens up properly as we get closer to release.

Narrative Writer - 1/1 position

Writing the campaign and narrative content across the entire project - missions, side missions, backstory, all of it. You'd be building outward from existing concept art and narrative material already established, working closely with me to keep everything aligned, but with a lot of genuine creative freedom in how missions and story beats come together. This one touches nearly every other part of the project - the locations builders create, dialogue for the enemies the modeller builds - so it's one of the most creatively open roles on the team.

Something Else? - Open

If you think you've got something to offer that isn't covered by anything above, this one's for you. Instead of fixed requirements, the application just asks what you'd want to do, why, what that would actually look like in practice, and any evidence or portfolio to back it up. No guarantee a spot exists for it yet - but if it's a genuine fit, we'll figure out where it goes.

We've already got our first result from the new pipeline: Yellowy joins us as a Server Developer, and is currently underway building a custom weapon plugin from scratch.

The last one is the Main Menu itself - the one I've been most hesitant to finalise since things keep changing, but I do believe this is close to the final version now. Since a lot of these haven't been talked about at all yet, here's a quick walkthrough of what each button actually does:

[Main Menu screenshot goes here]

  • Season Pass - takes you to the Seasonal Hub, where you can access the pass itself, purchase the premium pass, and access seasonal challenges.
  • Quests - access all your active quests.
  • Bounties - access all your active bounties.
  • Triumph - view your triumphs and claim triumph rewards.
  • Titles - view and equip your earned titles.
  • Collections - everything you've discovered across Sol - lore pages you've unlocked and can read, locations you've visited (some of which you can teleport to), enemies you've defeated and their drop tables, and more.
  • Inspect - a detailed look at a weapon's stats and perks.
  • Updates - highlights and patch notes for previous server updates and announcements.
  • Trade - trade with other players, or buy and sell items.
  • Store - the Eververse store, containing cosmetics and items from past seasons.
  • Level - view your level.
  • Armour - view your armour stats.
  • Currency - view how much of all your currencies and engrams you have.
  • Subclass - view your class, and view or change your subclass.
  • Vault - access your vault virtually.
  • Timeline - a timeline of major story moments across the world, filling out as you complete missions, quests, and discover story beats.
  • Clan Hall - join or create a clan, view your clan, invite members, and more.
  • Stats - a collection of lifetime stats, plus the top 3 players on the server for each one.
  • Guide - the guidebook, covering all the important server features, mechanics, and systems.
  • Change Character - change character.

Destiny Is Coming Back

Slightly unrelated to us directly, but worth saying: Destiny was announced to be coming back, with the original campaigns being unvaulted. Genuinely exciting news, and I wanted to acknowledge it here rather than pretend it didn't happen.

To be clear though - this doesn't change anything about Destiny Playground, and if anything it makes what we're doing more interesting. We're not trying to be a copy of the original campaigns; Destiny Playground has always been about telling our own alternate, unique story within this world. That approach only gets more interesting alongside the real thing coming back, not less.

Where the Team Stands

Being straightforward with you: right now it's looking like myself and Yellowy are the only active staff members on the team. Emi, Unknown, and lilkorio are officially taking an extended break, and the rest is currently unknown. So please excuse it if progress looks a little slower than usual for a while - there's genuinely just two of us actively working at the moment. Hopefully the new roles pipeline starts changing that soon.

That's everything for this one. Thanks, as always, for sticking with us.
