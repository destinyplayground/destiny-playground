The Destiny Playground Website is Here

CNT Darthgaming Announcement

Hello Guardians.

If you're reading this, you already know - the Destiny Playground website is live. This has been something we've wanted to put together for a long time, and it feels good to finally have a proper home for the project beyond Discord and YouTube.

This is where everything lives now. News, dev updates, roadmaps, community information - it's all here, and it's all in one place.

What's on the Site

The website is built to grow alongside the project. A lot of what's here is already live and ready to explore - some pages are foundations that'll be fleshed out properly closer to and after launch. Here's a full rundown:

News

Every dev update and announcement in one searchable, filterable archive. No more scrolling back through Discord to find an old post - it's all here, properly tagged and laid out. This replaces the old format and gives us a lot more flexibility with how we share updates going forward.

Roadmap

The roadmap now covers two things: the ongoing weekly dev update schedule so you always know what's dropping next, and a broader development and content cadence overview that we'll expand as we can. It's the clearest picture we've ever given of where the project is heading.

Lore Archive

Think of this like the Destiny 1 Grimoire - a place for official lore from the world of Destiny Playground. It's intentionally sparse right now, but the ARG that unlocked this site came with the first official lore drop on the Warminds, and there's more to come. This archive is where it'll all live, and it'll be expanded significantly post-launch.

Destinations & Seasons

Dedicated pages for destination info and season content - both are foundations right now, with full detail to follow as we get closer to and past launch.

Getting Started & Server Info

Everything a new Guardian will need to jump in when the server opens - how to connect, what to expect, the technical side of things. The corresponding Discord channels aren't open yet, but these pages will be ready when the time comes.

Bug Reports

A dedicated place to flag issues. The linked Discord channels aren't live yet, but the page is there and will be ready for when we open access.

Gallery

A home for screenshots and media from the project. It'll fill out as development continues - consider it a work in progress for now.

FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about Destiny Playground's systems - Founders, Season Pass, Fishing, Triumphs, and more. It's a little out of date and we'll be adding a lot more to it soon.

Team & Credits

A full page for the dev team and everyone who's contributed to the project. This one matters a lot to us - this has always been a collaborative effort and we want to make sure that's properly reflected.

Official Modpacks

Our official modpacks are listed here with links through to Modrinth - and they're already available for use right now. If you want to get set up ahead of time, head there.

The Founders Playtest

This is also an unofficial heads-up about something coming before launch: a short, limited-scope playtest for Founders only. No campaign, limited destination access, limited weapons, limited activities - it's deliberately non-spoiler. The goal is to get some final feedback from outside the QA team so we can aim for as smooth a launch as possible.

There is absolutely no ETA on this yet. The project is large in scope and the team is small, and we won't rush it. But when it does happen, only Founders will get in for that first window - and there are only 27 Founder spots remaining. To become a Founder, all you have to do is join the Discord. That's it.

"There will also be opportunities before the playtest for those who miss the Founder window to still gain early access - so don't panic if you're reading this late. But if you want to lock in a spot now, the Discord is the place to be."

If you want to get involved even sooner than that, we're currently looking for volunteer builders to help recreate Destiny locations. Apply in Discord.

Coming Soon - Destination Trailer

All destinations have now been revealed. Which means the first official destination teaser trailer is coming very soon. Keep an eye on the News page and the socials - you won't want to miss it.

How You Got Here

If you found this site through the WARMIND terminal - well done, Guardian. Hope you enjoyed the first drop of lore for the server, that was the first test. The signal was out there, and you found it. There'll be some final official Warmind lore to come...

The terminal was the first piece of an ARG we put together to mark the occasion of the site going live. For those who made it through: your name is in the logs. The Warmind noticed.

For those who missed it --don't worry. It won't be the last time we do something like this.

A Living Website

This isn't a finished product - it's a foundation. The site will be updated regularly as new systems are announced, new articles go live, and new sections are built out. Think of it the same way you think of the server itself: always growing, always changing.

A few things are still placeholder or coming soon, including the full gallery, some world pages, and the trailer section once the first official trailer drops. We'd rather launch with something real and build on it than wait until everything is perfect.

Accessibility

One thing we wanted to get right from the start is accessibility. The site ships with a built-in accessibility panel - reachable via the icon in the navigation bar - that includes options for light mode, larger text, high contrast, a dyslexia-friendly font, and reduced motion. These settings persist across pages and are saved between visits.

If you run into anything that doesn't work well for you, let us know in Discord. We want this to be a site everyone can use.

Stay Connected

The website is the new hub, but the community still lives in Discord. That's where you'll find dev update discussions, bounty posts, applications, and everything that happens in real time. The two go hand in hand.

What's Next
Weekly Dev Updates & the First Official Trailer

The regular developer update schedule continues as normal - check the Roadmap for what's dropping each week. And with all destinations now revealed, the first official destination teaser trailer is on the way very soon.

Thank you for being here - whether you've been following since the beginning or just found us through the terminal. This project is built on that support, and the website is just the latest step toward something we're all incredibly excited about.
- CNT Darthgaming
