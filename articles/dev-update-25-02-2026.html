How Weapons Function in TDP

Impact (Damage)

Impact determines how much damage the weapon deals per shot against enemies. At 8.25 Impact, Halfdan-D fits the High-Impact archetype - slower firing, heavier hitting, and more deliberate in feel.

Range (Measured in Blocks)

Range is measured in Minecraft blocks. Halfdan-D is hitscan, meaning shots register instantly, no projectile entity is spawned, there's no gravity arc, and it runs with much better performance efficiency. Some weapon types (such as grenade launchers) will use projectile systems with gravity - however, most standard weapons use hitscan to maintain responsiveness and server performance.

Magazine and Reload System

Halfdan-D has a 33-round magazine. Instead of a traditional ammo counter UI, the Minecraft durability bar represents ammunition. Each shot reduces durability; when the magazine empties, the weapon automatically reloads. During reload, the weapon cannot be fired, and the durability bar visibly refills as the reload completes. Players can also manually reload by crouching (this may change later).

Additional Weapon Stats

  • Stability - Controls recoil and camera movement after firing
  • Handling - Determines ready speed after switching weapons
  • Reload Speed - Affects reload duration
  • Zoom - Influences ADS behaviour
  • Level Requirement - Based on rarity

Controls: Left click to fire  |  Right click to aim down sights (ADS)

Perk Structure

Legendary weapons feature five perks: a Scope perk, Magazine perk, two Damage / Elemental perks, and an Origin perk. For Halfdan-D:

1 GB Iron Scope
2 Flared Magwell Magazine
3 Outlaw Perk
4 Rampage Perk
5 Hakke Breach Armaments Origin

Where possible, Destiny's version of perks will be recreated as closely as possible. However, some perks may function slightly differently due to Minecraft constraints, and others may be entirely unique to TDP. Weapons will have several versions that can drop with different perk combinations - the weapon above is just 1 of Halfdan-D's 4 possible rolls.

Frame Identity: High-Impact

Frames function similarly to Destiny 2 archetypes. High-Impact Auto Rifles fire slower (360 RPM), deal higher damage per shot, and encourage controlled, deliberate engagements. The goal is to preserve the identity of familiar archetypes while ensuring they feel good and balanced within Minecraft's constraints.

"Halfdan-D is the first weapon to fully run through our completed weapon pipeline. There may still be tuning and system adjustments, but the foundation is complete."
