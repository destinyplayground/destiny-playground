Community Initiative
Community Survey - Now Live

The first official Destiny Playground community survey is now live. This project is ambitious, long-term, and heavily community-shaped, and this survey is a major step in defining launch structure, progression systems, live world direction, post-launch expansion priorities, and long-term system refinement.

It takes on average 15 minutes, is completely anonymous, and includes context throughout - so even if you're not deeply familiar with Destiny-style systems, you can still meaningfully contribute. No single answer will determine direction. Patterns and trends will.

Stats Menu

The new Stats Menu allows players to view detailed lifetime statistics - not just for fun, but to introduce a light competitive edge to the server.

Players can track stats such as:

  • Player kills
  • Mob kills
  • Distance walked
  • Fish caught
  • Damage dealt
  • Damage taken
  • And more...

Not only can you see your own statistics, but each category also displays the Top 5 players on the server, adding a subtle competitive layer without making it the core focus.

Timeline

The Timeline Menu acts as a structured recap system for story progression. It functions similarly to Destiny 2's timeline, but in a more detailed format.

As you complete campaigns and storylines, key moments will unlock and appear on your timeline, which allows players to:

  • Recap major narrative events
  • Track story progression
  • Revisit important milestones
  • Maintain context between releases

The goal here is long-term clarity. As the world expands and multiple campaigns release over time, players won't lose track of what has happened.

Planet 2 Reveal
Europa

Congratulations to those who spotted the teaser hidden in the website footer and those that correctly guessed in the Discord!

Planet 2 is officially revealed as Europa.

Europa will act as the second Patrol Zone in Destiny Playground. For the most part, this destination will visually resemble Destiny 2's Europa, with a few important differences:

  • The Pyramid is not present
  • Certain structures will differ
  • Some areas have been adapted

Development Progress

Europa is currently largely complete from a world-building perspective. What remains:

  • Lost Sectors
  • The associated Strike
  • A few additional features that aren't ready to be revealed

Destination Structure

Europa will feature:

  • 3 Landing Zones
  • 4 Primary Combat Zones
  • 1 Social Zone - Charon's Crossing, home to the planetary vendor

As with Earth, the world-view map artwork is not yet complete, but will be added once available.

Development Progress This Week

Development this week has been very strong behind the scenes. Work has begun on what's being called Campaign and Structure Overview V2.

After creating the original campaign document several months ago, there's been time to reflect on its strengths and weaknesses. This new version focuses less on isolated story beats and more on the overall structural flow - how content connects, how progression unfolds, and how players move through the world long-term.

This isn't something that needs immediate public detail, but it's foundational work that will shape everything going forward. You'll begin hearing more about the updated structure, along with a high-level campaign overview, in May.
