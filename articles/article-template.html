The Playground Takes Shape — First Dev Update

28 Mar, 2026
CNT Darthgaming Dev Update

Welcome to the very first official dev update for Destiny Playground. If you're reading this, you're one of the earliest people to follow this project — and that means a lot. This update covers where we are, what's been done, and where we're heading next.

Where We Are

The foundations are in place. The concept has been locked in, the website is live, and work has officially begun on the opening chapter of the Destiny Playground experience.

"This isn't just a Minecraft server. It's a world. And we're just getting started building it."

What's In Progress

Opening Cutscene

The script for the opening cutscene is written and ready. Voice acting is being recorded using a custom Ghost filter effect built in Audacity.
