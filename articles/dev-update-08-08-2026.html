The Sparrow System

Status
Working Prototype - Not Currently a Priority

Functional in-game already, but still early. No screenshots or footage yet, and no Sparrow models exist currently either.

Sparrows live as an item in your inventory or hotbar. Use the item anywhere and it spawns into the world, ready to ride. The intent is for it to be a genuinely faster alternative to walking, and we want to make sure of that - riding a Sparrow needs to actually feel worth it, not just be a novelty.

That said, summoning and stowing one isn't instant - there's a deliberate time cost to pulling it out and putting it away, which walking doesn't have. That's by design: if a Sparrow were both instant to deploy and faster than walking with no downside, there'd be no reason to ever walk anywhere again, which isn't the experience we want. The time cost is what keeps the trade-off honest - use a Sparrow for real distance, walk when you're just popping somewhere close.

Shift right-click your Sparrow to open its menu. From there you can either pick it back up, or apply upgrades - improving attributes like max speed, acceleration, handling, brake power, traction, and turning. Nothing about the specific numbers or upgrade costs is locked in yet - that'll come later, once the core system's been tested properly.

There'll also be a range of different Sparrows to unlock, both for upgrade potential (rarer Sparrows come with better ceilings on those stats) and for pure customisation - so even once you've got a Sparrow you're happy with performance-wise, there's still reason to chase others just for how they look. As mentioned, we don't have any Sparrow models built currently, so there's nothing to show visually yet - but we're hoping to get some made eventually. This system just isn't the current priority, so it'll sit here as a working prototype until it is.
