CNT Darthgaming Dev Update

Welcome back, Guardians. It's been a while — longer than any of us wanted. Before anything else, I want to take a moment to be honest about where the last few weeks went, and say a proper thank you for the patience shown while things went quiet.

A Note on the Silence

The gap since the last update on 8th June is entirely on me. I was in the middle of an intense three-week teaching internship, which on its own would have been manageable — but paired with over four hours of commuting every single day, there was simply nothing left by the time I got home. Not one, but two heatwaves across that period made things harder still. I had no time, no energy, and no headspace to give the project what it deserved. I'm personally sorry for the radio silence, and for the knock-on delay to the listed dev updates and the Ability Teaser Trailer. You stuck around anyway. That genuinely means a lot.

The good news: we're moving again. And there's actually quite a lot to cover — because while the updates went quiet, some things didn't stop. Let's get into it.

Weapons — The Biggest Progress Update Yet

This is the section that's had the most work put into it, and there's a lot to cover. The weapon system has matured significantly — not just in terms of what exists, but in how it's built and documented.

A major internal effort went into building a comprehensive guide and process for creating weapons — how decisions are made, how Destiny 2 weapons translate into Minecraft, how each element is scoped and built. It's long, it's detailed, and it means that whether we've been heads-down on weapons for months or just coming back after a break, we have something to return to. This alone will accelerate progress from here significantly.

Beyond that, the underlying systems have been refined. Our perk-building process is considerably better, and we've been compiling a full list of perks to work from — D2 conversions, unique Minecraft-native ones, and potentially some from Destiny 1 and Destiny Rising too. The random roll system has also had internal improvements, and one notable quality-of-life change: weapon stats now reflect perk modifications inline — if a perk adds two rounds to the magazine, the description will now show something like 12+2. Small detail, big difference.

We've also been rethinking how weapons sit in your hand. There's a balance between being able to actually see the model and not having it fill the screen — and we've landed in a place we're genuinely happy with.

What's Next — A Shift in Focus

Going forward, my personal focus is shifting more toward development and coding over building. With lilkorio joining to push The Moon forward, that side of things is covered. Weapon models are flowing in. And I expect the broader team to ease back into a good rhythm over the coming weeks.

A Quick Note on the Website

The site launched just under two months ago, and the reception has been really positive — genuinely glad it landed well. That said, we've been looking at it critically and there's a feeling that the colours, fonts, and overall theme don't quite reflect Destiny's aesthetic as well as they should. We're working on a rework of a lot of that — it'll roll out gradually, one page at a time over a long period, so don't be surprised if things shift around. We'd love your input on what direction that goes, so head to Discord and share your thoughts while it's in progress.
