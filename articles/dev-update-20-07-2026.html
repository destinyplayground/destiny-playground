Weapons - A Lot Has Happened

This is the area with the most to cover. The biggest thing isn't a weapon itself - it's the process behind them. I've put a significant amount of time into building a detailed internal guide for how weapons are made: how decisions get made, how D2 weapons translate into Minecraft, what the structure looks like at every step. It's long, it's thorough, and it means that whether we've been deep in weapons for weeks or coming back after a break, we have something solid to return to. That alone will speed things up considerably from here.

Beyond that - the perk-building process is a lot better now, our random roll system has had some internal refinements, and one notable change: weapon stats now reflect what perks do to them inline. If a perk adds two rounds to the magazine, the stat shows something like 12+2. Small thing, but it matters. We've also put some thought into how weapons actually sit in your hand - finding the balance between actually being able to see the model and not having it take up the whole screen. We're happy with where it is now.

A Quick Website Note

The site has been live for just under two months now, and the reception has been really positive - genuinely glad it landed well. That said, we've been looking at it more critically and feel the colours, fonts, and general theme don't quite reflect Destiny's aesthetic the way they should. We're working on reworking a lot of that. It'll roll out gradually over a long period - you might notice pages shifting one at a time as we go. We'd love to hear your thoughts on the direction that takes, so come share them in the Discord.
