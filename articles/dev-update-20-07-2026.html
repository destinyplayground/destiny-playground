Weapons

A lot has happened here. The biggest thing isn't a specific weapon - it's the process. I've put a significant amount of time into building a detailed internal guide for how weapons are made: how decisions get made, how D2 weapons translate into Minecraft, the full structure at each step. Having that documented means we're no longer relying on memory when we come back to something after a break, and it means the process is consistent regardless of who's working on what. That's going to speed things up a lot.

On top of that - the perk-building process is much better now, the random roll system has had some internal changes, and weapon stats now reflect what perks do to them inline. If a perk adds two rounds to the magazine, the description will show something like 12+2. We've also put work into how weapons sit in your hand - the balance between actually being able to see the model and not having it fill the screen. Happy with where it is now.

A Note on the Website

The site's been live for just under two months now, and it's had a great reception - really glad it landed the way it did. That said, looking back, we felt the colours, fonts, and overall theme didn't reflect Destiny's aesthetic as well as they should, so we've started rolling out a rework.

Right now that rollout only covers the home page, articles, and general site-wide formatting - most other pages may still be running the old fonts and colours for a while yet, so don't be alarmed if things look inconsistent between pages. This is a long, gradual rollout done one piece at a time, not an overnight switch.

Layouts haven't changed yet - this pass is colours and fonts only - but layout tweaks are planned down the line too.

One heads up: during this transition, accessibility features may not behave as intended on some pages. Apologies in advance for that - we're working through it.

We'd love to hear what you think about the direction, so come share your thoughts in the Discord.
