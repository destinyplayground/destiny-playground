Ability Structure

Each subclass provides access to four core abilities. These can be used at any time via key binds and do not take up inventory slots.

  • Grenade
  • Melee
  • Class Ability
  • Super

Each class & subclass will have a selection of abilities to choose from:

  • 3 Grenades
  • 2 Melees
  • 2 Class Abilities
  • 2 Supers

Abilities can be freely swapped through a menu, allowing players to adjust their build at any time.

Subclasses

At launch, each class will have access to the 3 Light subclasses. Dark subclasses are not planned for launch.

Aspects & Fragments have been considered, but will not be included at launch. This may be revisited post-launch depending on how it fits. The goal is to keep subclasses clear, impactful, & easy to build around — without overcomplicating the system early on.

Unlocking Subclasses & Abilities

When starting out, players will unlock their first subclass early on at a basic level, with a limited set of abilities. Progression is structured as follows:

  • First subclass unlocked early in the campaign
  • Second subclass unlocked later in the campaign
  • Third subclass unlocked post-campaign
  • Each subclass also has its own dedicated upgrade quest, which unlocks the remaining abilities for that subclass

Rather than unlocking abilities individually in any order, this system is designed to be more structured & streamlined.

Ability Progression — Rarity System

Abilities will have their own rarity tiers, similar to gear progression. Each subclass starts at Common and can be upgraded through repeatable activities, quests, or tasks.

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Legendary → Exotic

Upgrading a subclass increases the effectiveness of its abilities, including things like damage, effects, and cooldown. This ensures abilities scale alongside the player's progression, rather than remaining static or falling out of balance.

Ability Selection

Abilities are managed through the Character Menu. From here, players can:

  • Equip a subclass
  • Inspect that subclass
  • Swap abilities
  • View ability details & rarities

This system is designed to make build crafting accessible & flexible, while still allowing depth.

Current State & Future Updates

This system is still in development, and while the core direction is mostly set, details may still change as it moves into implementation. Once UI development begins, future updates will include menu designs, ability showcases, and subclass breakdowns.

We are currently aiming to share UI & ability showcases around May, if development timelines allow. Concept work will be shared on our Ko-fi before then.
