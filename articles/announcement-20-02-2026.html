Community Participation Opportunities

20 Feb, 2026
CNT Darthgaming Announcement

As development continues, we're opening more ways for the community to get involved and contribute to the project. This has always been a passion-driven space, and we want participation to feel exciting, rewarding, and collaborative.

We're currently opening:

  • Volunteer Builder Applications
  • Volunteer Modeller Applications
  • Community Bounties System
Open Applications
Volunteer Builders
  • Strong portfolio of previous Minecraft builds (required)
  • Must own Minecraft Java Edition, version 1.21.8
  • Must have access to Destiny 1 and/or Destiny 2 for reference
  • Familiarity with Destiny's environments and aesthetic
  • Ability to build detailed environments with or without external tools
Open Applications
Volunteer Modellers
  • Portfolio of previous work (required)
  • Experience using Blockbench
  • Access to Destiny for visual reference
  • Weapon and item models
  • Enemy models with animations

Builder Applications

We're looking for high-skill builders who can help bring locations to life. This role is best suited for people who are confident in their ability to build.

Knowledge of tools such as WorldEdit or Axiom is appreciated, but not required. These tools will be available - however, we are also specifically looking for builders who can produce high-quality work even without relying on them. Builds created with only external importing tools or Axiom will be rejected.

"Applications without a portfolio will be declined. Destiny-inspired past builds increase acceptance chances. If you don't have prior examples, you have the opportunity to create a Destiny build and re-apply - for example, creating a crucible map."

Modeller Applications

We're also recruiting 3D modellers to help create custom assets. Examples of what you may work on include weapon models, item models, enemy models with animations, and furniture models.

Community Bounties System

Inspired by the system once used by TCMC, we're introducing our own Bounty System - a flexible way for anyone to contribute without needing to commit to a full role. This system replaces our previous open submission method so we can better request exactly what the project currently needs.

◆   Bounty System
How It Works
  • We post a task
  • You complete it
  • You earn Bounty Points
  • Points accumulate on a leaderboard
Example Bounties
  • Create a texture
  • Model a weapon or item
  • Model an enemy
  • Build a small location
Potential Rewards
  • Early access opportunities
  • Contributor recognition
  • Potential additional perks

This system is especially great for those who want to help but don't have time to commit to a full position.

Get Involved
Applications and Bounties - Via Discord

Applications and bounty posts will be available within the coming week, accessible exclusively via our Discord server. Head there to find out more.

This project is built on passion, creativity and the overall community. Every contribution, big or small, helps shape the world and gets us a step closer to release.

Thank you for being part of this. As always, every bit of support - whether that's contributing directly, sharing the project, or simply following along - means the world.
- CNT Darthgaming
