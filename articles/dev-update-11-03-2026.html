Developer Note

The purpose of these dev updates is to share systems currently in development and to give insight into the early stages of design as the project evolves. What you're seeing right now are the foundations and skeletons of systems, nowhere near their final versions.

Things like names, values, stats, and organisation are often placeholders or early iterations. My current focus is building the core structure and functionality of systems first. Refinement, balancing, and polish come later in development.

Because of this, please keep in mind that everything shown in dev updates is subject to change. Systems may be adjusted, expanded, renamed, reorganised, or even removed entirely as development continues. Feedback and suggestions are always welcome, but constructive feedback with reasoning is far more helpful than simple reactions. It's also worth remembering that seeing a system from the outside doesn't always show the full context of how it connects with other systems or the wider project.

Character Creation

When you first join the server, you will create a Character Profile which acts as your persistent identity within Destiny Playground. Each character maintains its own progression, including:

  • Gear and equipment
  • Campaign progress
  • Character level
  • Currencies
  • Progression systems

At launch, players will be able to create up to three characters, similar to Destiny. A fourth character slot will be available for purchase.

Unlike Destiny, however, most systems in Destiny Playground are tracked per character rather than per account. This means that currencies, gear, and progression will largely remain unique to the character you are currently playing. The exact details of what does and does not transfer between characters are still being finalised.

Players will be able to switch between their characters at any time without leaving the server, making it easy to play multiple classes. Characters can also be deleted at any time, though this action is irreversible.

Each character also has its own Class Level progression. At launch we are currently aiming for Level 100 as the maximum level, though this may be adjusted depending on campaign progression, experience scaling, and long-term progression goals.

Classes

Destiny Playground will feature the same three core classes found in Destiny:

  • Titan
  • Hunter
  • Warlock

Each class focuses on a distinct playstyle and set of abilities, giving players different ways to approach combat and gameplay.

One major difference from Destiny involves armour usage. In Destiny Playground, most armour is not restricted by class. All classes share the same general armour pool, allowing players far greater flexibility when building their characters. The only exception to this rule is Exotic armour, which remains class-specific.

Armour System

Armour in Destiny Playground works quite differently from Destiny's system. There are still six core armour stats, but each stat influences multiple gameplay attributes rather than a single mechanic.

The six core stats are: Strength, Intellect, Wisdom, Dexterity, Intelligence, and Mobility.

Strength
  • Physical Damage
  • Attack Speed
  • Block Rating
Intellect
  • Maximum Health
  • Health Regeneration
  • Knockback Resistance
Wisdom
  • Maximum Mana
  • Mana Regeneration
  • Skill Cooldown Reduction
Dexterity
  • Weapon Damage
  • Projectile Damage
  • Block Break Speed
  • Parry Rating
Intelligence
  • Critical Strike Chance
  • Critical Strike Power
  • Dodge Rating
Mobility
  • Movement Speed
  • Sneaking Speed
  • Jump Strength
  • Additional Experience Gain

Armour Rolls

Similar to Destiny 2, Legendary armour pieces will roll with different stat distributions. Typically, a Legendary armour piece will include one primary stat with a high number of points, one secondary stat with a moderate number of points, and two additional stats with smaller bonuses. This ensures armour pieces feel unique and encourages players to build specific stat combinations. Armour will also eventually roll with perks, similar to weapons - more details will be shared in a future update.

Class Identity

Each class will have natural strengths and weaknesses within the stat system. These act as baseline modifiers that reinforce each class's identity and playstyle, while still allowing players to customise their builds through gear, stats, and abilities.

Titan
+ Strength
+ Intelligence
- Intellect
- Mobility
Hunter
+ Intelligence
+ Mobility
- Wisdom
- Dexterity
Warlock
+ Intellect
+ Wisdom
- Strength
- Intelligence

Subclasses and Abilities

Destiny Playground will include an ability system inspired by Destiny's subclass structure. Each class will have access to a Grenade Ability, Melee Ability, Class Ability, and Super Ability. These can be used without occupying inventory slots, allowing players to use their abilities naturally alongside their weapons.

One difference from Destiny is that grenades are also class-specific in Destiny Playground. Each class will have access to three unique grenades:

Titan Grenades
  • Incendiary Grenade
  • Solar Grenade
  • Fusion Grenade
Hunter Grenades
  • Tripmine Grenade
  • Thermite Grenade
  • Swarm Grenade
Warlock Grenades
  • Healing Grenade
  • Molten Grenade
  • Firebolt Grenade

We will explore abilities, subclasses, and combat mechanics in much greater detail in the next development update.
